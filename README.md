# MyPillTime Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 9 September 2026**

MyPillTime is a medication management application designed to help users organise medication information, schedule reminders, monitor stock and expiry dates, and record doses taken.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information MyPillTime stores, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

## 1. Information Stored by MyPillTime

MyPillTime allows you to enter and store information relating to your medications, including:

* Medication name
* Dosage
* Scheduled medication time
* Instructions
* Stock quantity
* Low-stock warning level
* Expiry date
* Medication photographs
* Records of doses that you have recorded as taken

This information may constitute personal or health-related information because it can reveal information about a person's medications.

## 2. Where Your Information Is Stored

MyPillTime stores medication information and dose history locally on your device using the application's local storage.

The current version of MyPillTime does not provide an account-based cloud storage service for this information and does not upload your medication records or dose history to a MyPillTime server.

Because this information is stored on your device, it may be accessible to anyone who has access to your device or its contents.

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

The information you enter is used to provide the features of MyPillTime, including:

* Displaying your medications
* Showing medication details
* Scheduling medication reminders
* Calculating stock and low-stock warnings
* Identifying expired medications
* Recording doses taken
* Displaying dose history
* Showing medication and adherence information within the app

MyPillTime does not use your medication information for advertising or behavioural profiling.

## 4. Notifications

MyPillTime may request permission to send notifications so that medication reminders can be delivered at the times you specify.

Notification content may include the name of the medication you entered and a reminder that it is time to take the medication.

You can enable or disable medication reminders from the Settings section of MyPillTime. You can also control notification permissions through your Android device settings.

When a medication reminder is tapped, MyPillTime may open the corresponding medication within the application so that you can review it and decide whether to record the dose.

## 5. Photographs and Camera Access

MyPillTime allows you to take or select a photograph for a medication.

Camera and photo-library permissions are requested only when you choose to use these features.

Medication photographs selected for use in MyPillTime are stored as part of the application's local data on your device.

## 6. Data Sharing

MyPillTime does not sell your medication information, dose history, medication photographs, or other information that you enter into the application.

The current version of MyPillTime does not provide a feature that sends your medication records or dose history to healthcare professionals, carers, advertisers, or other third parties.

## 7. Data Retention

Your medication information and dose history remain stored on your device until you delete them from the application or remove the application's stored data.

Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and Android settings.

## 8. Deleting Your Data

MyPillTime is intended to provide users with control over their locally stored medication information.

The application may provide a **Clear All Data** function that removes stored medication information and dose history from the application.

Deleting stored data is permanent and cannot be undone unless you have made your own backup.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the application and its local data handling appropriate for its intended use.

Because MyPillTime currently stores information locally on your device rather than in a MyPillTime cloud account, the security of the device itself is also important. We recommend using the security features provided by your device, such as a screen lock.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Children's Privacy

MyPillTime is not specifically directed at children.

Users should use MyPillTime in accordance with applicable laws and the requirements of their device and app store account.

## 11. Health and Medical Information

MyPillTime is a medication organisation and reminder tool. It is **not a medical device** and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

MyPillTime does not provide medical advice, prescribe medication, determine whether a medication is appropriate for you, or recommend changes to prescribed treatment.

The information entered into MyPillTime is provided and managed by the user and should not be relied upon as a substitute for advice from a doctor, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare professional.

Always follow the medication instructions provided by your healthcare professional.

If you are unsure whether you should take a medication, what dose you should take, or how your medication should be used, consult a doctor, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare professional.


## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to MyPillTime, its features, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this location with a new **Last updated** date.
